# iOS App Icon And Brand System

Strong brand does not need to fight the platform.

## Icon Rules

- keep the silhouette simple
- design for tiny sizes first
- avoid text if possible
- prefer one strong symbol or shape
- test against home screen contexts, not just on white artboards
- use strong contrast without becoming noisy
- assume Apple applies masking to exported art

## App Store Icon Review

Review in this order:

1. small-size clarity
2. silhouette and recognizability
3. contrast in light and dark surroundings
4. distinctiveness against category competitors
5. consistency with the product, screenshots, and listing promise

- one mark beats a scene
- simple geometry beats tiny detail
- bold color direction beats muddy gradients
- test the icon at realistic home-screen and App Store sizes

## Brand System Rules

- one primary accent color
- one calm background strategy
- one clear typographic hierarchy
- consistent icon treatment across product and marketing

## Avoid

- over-detailed icons
- raw bitmap edges where masking assumptions matter
- one brand style in-app and another in screenshots
- branding so strong it breaks native trust
- an icon that only works on a large artboard
